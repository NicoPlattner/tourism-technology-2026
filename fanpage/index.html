Things to do

The valley

Four villages, four moods

Ischgl 1,377 m

The famous one. Big ski area, big concerts, lively evenings — and very good food.

Galtür 1,584 m

Highest and quietest. Wide valley floor, hiking, ski touring and the Alpinarium.

Kappl 1,258 m

Sunny terraces and a relaxed, family-friendly ski area of its own.

See ≈ 1,050 m

The gateway to the valley. Small and friendly, with its own ski area and a 6 km toboggan run.
